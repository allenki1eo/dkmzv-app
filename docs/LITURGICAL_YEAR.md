# Liturgical colours (KKKT / Western Lutheran)

DKMZV **locks the UI cloth** to the church year. Gold `#D4AF37` is always the metal (cross, rules), never a season of its own.

| Vestment | Hex (DKMZV) | Seasons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Purple** | `#2E0854` | **Advent (Majilio)** and **Lent (Kwaresima)** — repentance, waiting, kingship |
| **Green** | `#1E4D36` (+ sage `#A2AD91`) | **Time after Epiphany** and **Time after Pentecost** — growth, ordinary discipleship |
| **White / gold** | cream `#F4E6C1` + gold | **Christmas, Easter, Trinity, All Saints** — joy, resurrection, glory |
| **Red** | `#8B1E2D` | **Pentecost, Palm Sunday, Good Friday, Reformation**, confirmation/ordination days — Spirit and blood |

Computed locally (no network) from Gregorian Easter. Preview a vestment from **Zaidi → Mwaka wa kanisa** without changing the calendar.
